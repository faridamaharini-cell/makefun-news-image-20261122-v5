# Sunday 22 November 2026: 37 stories, 7 regions

A one-page newspaper front page summing up Sunday 22 November 2026's world
news in a single image: 37 verified stories across 7 regions, with three
illustrated lead stories (186 drones over Ukraine, the COP31 deal landing
with no mention of fossil fuels, Iran's radar claim vs Bahrain's denial),
12 more illustrated stories, a dotted world map with numbered pins, a UTC
timeline, a numbers strip, and a DATE CHECK box that names the other days'
news left out on purpose. Every story happened, or was first reported, on
Sunday 22 November 2026 (UTC). Scenes are drawn illustrations, not
photographs.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG).
